# **App Name**: Global Connect Academy

## Core Features:

- Multilingual Content Management: Seamlessly switch all website content between French, English, and Simplified Chinese via an elegant selector, ensuring instant and fluid translation across all pages.
- Interactive Training Catalog Display: Present the 14 training courses categorized by Luxury & Hospitality, Sport & Management, Languages & International, and Business & Strategy using interactive cards within a grid or carousel.
- Immersive Homepage Experience: Feature a dynamic hero section with background video or a 3D animated globe illustrating connections between France, China, and Southeast Asia to capture immediate attention.
- Dynamic Student Testimonials: Showcase a slider of testimonials from former French and Chinese students, providing social proof and inspiring prospective enrollees.
- Multilingual Contact & Map: Provide a responsive contact form and an interactive map, both localized, allowing visitors to easily inquire and locate the academy.
- Elegant Micro-interactions & Scroll Reveals: Incorporate subtle animations for buttons on hover, scroll-triggered fade-up effects for text and images, and 3D micro-animations on training cards (e.g., stylized aircraft seat for 'Flight Attendant').
- AI Course Content and Translation Tool: Leverage an AI tool to assist with drafting comprehensive descriptions for new courses or generating high-quality preliminary translations for marketing materials and web content in all supported languages.

## Style Guidelines:

- A refined color palette embodying luxury and global connectivity. The primary brand color is a deep Night Blue (#0B1A33), which conveys professionalism and sophistication. The background is a slightly tinted dark grey (#0C1014) to maintain depth while ensuring content readability in a dark scheme. A rich Gold (#C5A059) serves as the accent color, highlighting key interactive elements and titles with a touch of elegance.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for its precise, contemporary, and geometric appearance, enhancing the modern feel. Body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its neutral, highly legible, and objective readability across various content lengths and languages.
- Utilize sleek, minimalist line icons that align with themes of luxury, aviation, international travel, and education. Icons should be easily understandable across different cultures and contribute to a polished visual aesthetic.
- Emphasize an 'epuré' (clean) and spacious layout with ample white (or rather, 'dark space') to create a sense of luxury and clarity. Content is organized with clear visual hierarchy, using a responsive grid system that gracefully adapts for optimal viewing on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- Implement fluid and performant animations including immersive hero section movements (video or 3D globe), seamless language transitions, subtle hover effects on interactive elements, and graceful scroll-reveal animations (fade-up) for content. 3D elements (such as a spinning globe on the homepage or discreet course-specific micro-animations) enhance interactivity while remaining performant across devices.
